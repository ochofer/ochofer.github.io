Sole-authored measurement note from the data layer behind the climate-risk project. The ownership records that layer depends on are not stable between releases of the source database, and the note asks what a difference between two releases actually records.

The method is hand verification against primary sources: 19 of the 2,543 differences between two releases that the study classes as restatement, drawn in proportion to its three kinds, each checked against exchange filings, company statements and press releases. The note reports what share of them turns out to be restatement and what share new coverage.
